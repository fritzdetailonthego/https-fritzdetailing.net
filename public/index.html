Questions

Before you book.

For a special request or a vehicle outside the listed categories, call or text before checkout.

Where do appointments take place?

Fritz provides mobile service in Southwest Virginia. Confirm your location and any site requirements during booking.

How do I know the price?

The pricing section and checkout load from the current service menu. Your checkout total is calculated on the server from the selected services.

Can I pay online?

The checkout page shows the payment methods currently enabled by the business. Card payment is processed through Stripe when available.

What if my vehicle needs extra work?

Condition, distance, or special-request fees may be reviewed after booking. Any additions should be discussed with Fritz before the service.

How do I change or cancel an appointment?

Use the cancellation option provided with your booking when available, or call Fritz at (276) 247-0921.
