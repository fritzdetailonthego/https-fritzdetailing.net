A local point of contact for mobile vehicle detailing across Southwest Virginia.

The person behind the service

Fritz runs Fritz's Detail on the Go as a mobile operation. Customers choose their services, arrange a location, and work directly with the person doing the detail.

The current menu covers exterior care, interior cleaning, paint correction, ceramic coating, and packages for cars, SUVs, vans, and trucks. Availability and enabled payment options are shown in the online booking flow.

Built around convenience

Mobile service removes the shop drop-off from the process. Before the appointment, confirm that the location is suitable for the work and share anything Fritz should know about the vehicle.
