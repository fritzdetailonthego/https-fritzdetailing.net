Privacy

How booking information is used.

Last updated October 4, 2026.

Information you provide

When you use checkout or booking, the site may collect your name, phone number, email address, vehicle details, selected services, appointment details, service location, and notes you submit.

Payments

When card payment is available, payment details are entered into Stripe's payment form and processed by Stripe. The site receives payment status and transaction identifiers needed to connect payment with your order; it is not designed to store full card numbers.

How information is used

Information is used to create and manage orders, take payment, schedule and provide service, communicate about appointments, handle cancellations or adjustments, and maintain business records.

Sharing

Information may be shared with service providers that operate the website, payment processing, storage, or business communications, and when required by law. Customer information is not described here as being sold.

Retention and choices

Booking and transaction records may be kept as needed to operate the service and meet recordkeeping obligations. To ask about your information or request a correction or deletion where applicable, call (276) 247-0921. Some records may need to be retained for payment, tax, fraud prevention, or legal purposes.

External services

Links to Instagram, Google Maps, and other sites are governed by those services' privacy practices. This notice may be updated as the website and business tools change.
